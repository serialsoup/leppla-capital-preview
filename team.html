Our Team

A lean team, supported by a deep advisory network.

Leppla Capital is a family run firm that deliberately maintains a lean team while retaining a network of highly skilled economists, financial analysts, and statisticians. These professionals continuously evaluate domestic and international market trends and help identify high-value investment opportunities for our clients.

Leadership

Chief Investment Officer

Bruce W. Leppla

Bruce W. Leppla is the Chairman and Founder of Leppla Capital Management LLC. An economist and lawyer, he previously served as CEO and President of a San Francisco commercial bank, which he successfully sold at a substantial premium. Bruce holds a Juris Doctor and a master’s degree in economics from the University of California, Berkeley, and a B.A. in economics (magna cum laude) from Yale University.

Chief Strategy and Marketing Officer

Robert B. Leppla

Robert Bradford (“Brad”) Leppla serves as Chief Strategy & Marketing Officer at Leppla Capital. A former U.S. Navy aircraft carrier combat pilot who flew over 40 missions during Operation Desert Storm, Brad is also a computer engineer with extensive experience in enterprise technology systems. He holds a B.A. in forestry from the University of California, Berkeley, and an M.S. in computer science from Colorado Technical University.
