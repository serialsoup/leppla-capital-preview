What Guides Our Work

Fiduciary Duty

We are legally and ethically bound to put your interests ahead of our own at every step. There are no exceptions to this founding principle.

Long-Term Discipline

We design portfolios for resilience across full market cycles rather than short-term performance or headline risk. Capital preservation is not a slogan, it is the foundation of everything we do.

Aligned Incentives

We charge a straightforward fixed fee of 50 basis points, locked for a two-year period. As your assets grow, our fees remain the same. This structure removes any incentive to chase assets at the expense of suitability and keeps our interests aligned with your long-term success.

Education and Experience

We believe financial knowledge is one of the most powerful forms of wealth protection. Leppla Capital is committed to advancing financial literacy to help reduce income inequality through education.

Disciplined Investment Management with a Human Center.

At Leppla Capital, we believe superior long-term results come from combining rigorous analysis with genuine understanding of the people behind the capital. We retain a network of highly skilled economists, financial analysts, and statisticians who continuously monitor global economic developments, market trends, and technical indicators. This gives us timely insight into both risks and high-value opportunities while maintaining a clear-eyed, disciplined view of portfolio construction.

The firm was built on the principle that sophisticated investment management should be accessible, transparent, and aligned with the clients it serves.

Leppla Capital is a Delaware limited liability company registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation, and FINRA. We maintain full compliance in every jurisdiction we serve and operate with the highest standards of integrity and operational excellence.
