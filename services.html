Investment Approach

Our proprietary model.

The Securities Investment Pyramid shows how we think about risk: durable core holdings first, then more selective exposures sized carefully above them. Use the profile control to see how the foundation changes, or select a layer to understand its role.

Built from the Ground Up

A durable foundation. Selective growth above it.

At Leppla Capital, we believe the strongest portfolios begin with high-quality core holdings. More selective opportunities are added only after that foundation is in place, giving each client a disciplined balance between capital protection and long-term growth.

01Protect the foundation

Prioritize quality, liquidity, and broad diversification.

02Participate broadly

Capture market returns efficiently through diversified exposure.

03Pursue selectively

Add conviction only where the potential reward justifies the risk.

Putting the Framework into Practice

A repeatable process, monitored over time.

Broad diversification and selective conviction work together through a process that evolves with markets and client circumstances.

  1. 01Define the profile

    Clarify objectives, risk capacity, time horizon, and liquidity needs.

  2. 02Build the foundation

    Establish defensive holdings and broad market participation first.

  3. 03Add selective exposure

    Size focused opportunities according to conviction and client fit.

  4. 04Monitor and adjust

    Review the allocation as markets and client circumstances evolve.
